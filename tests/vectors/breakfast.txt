In the quiet of dawn, where morning light gleams,
A dance of culinary delight commences, it seems.

Breakfast awakes from its nightly repose,
To a symphony of flavors that weaves and flows.

Eggs, oh eggs, in their shelled seclusion,
Unveiled to the pan, with gentle introduction.

In the dance of heat, they reveal their art,
Their golden centers bursting from their heart.

But what's this? A twist to our breakfast tale,
Beans! The night owl of food, breaking the veil.

Not your typical bedfellow at dawn,
Yet here they are, bold and daringly drawn.

With beans in toast or perhaps a hearty stew,
Breakfast can't help but welcome this view.

The beans, once a dinner delight, take the stage,
In our breakfast production, refusing to engage.

Yet humbly they sit, with their earthy grace,
A witty addition to our morning embrace.

So here's to the morning, and its unusual fare,
A breakfast of eggs and beans, we now declare.

In a dance of flavors, it's quite rare,
But oh how delightful, when taken with care.
