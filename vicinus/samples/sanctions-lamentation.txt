In the realm where power once reigned unfettered,
A tale of sorrow now echoes unabated.

The iron fist that once gripped with unyielding might,
Now trembles under the weight of a troubled night.

A dictator's reign, once feared and obeyed,
Is now faced with sanctions, his pride frayed.

His treasures seized, his nation's wealth confiscated,
A cruel reminder that he was misdirected.

The people suffer, their pleas unheard,
In the name of freedom, a heavy toll is paid and rehearsed.

Yet, in this hour of darkness, there lies hope afar,
That one day, under a new star, they may live in peace and be spared.

For sanctions are but a call to reform,
A chance for change, a chance to transform.

Let the dictator's heart be softened by this plight,
And may his rule, once harsh and unbearable, take flight.

In the name of justice, let the sanctions prevail,
Till freedom rings, till tyranny does fail.
